# Product truth

## Purpose

Enable Opal (and later products) to safely:

- communicate with external systems
- coordinate company agents
- ingest durable streams
- carry payment authorization events (AVP² = **payments only**; device/social permissions are Opal-owned)
- execute and reconcile **payment** transactions
- retain replayable events
- rebuild projections
- support audit and proof
- support permitted analytics

## Human experience rule

Users never see Kafka, topics, offsets, brokers, or schema versions.

They experience reliable reservations, timely options, trustworthy payments, and calm Opal moments — delivered through Opal’s Phoenix edge after Opal validates social truth.

## Non-goals for Phase 0

- Production deployment
- Live agent calls
- Live payments
- Connection to production Opal databases
- Public social graphs
